## 3. Topic Discovery & Coherence Evaluation (NPMI)

### 3.1 Mathematical Definition of NPMI
Topic coherence is measured via **Normalized Pointwise Mutual Information (NPMI)** over the top-$M$ ($M=8$ to $10$) keywords:

$$\text{NPMI}(W) = \frac{1}{\binom{M}{2}} \sum_{i=1}^{M-1} \sum_{j=i+1}^M \frac{\ln \frac{P(w_i, w_j) + \epsilon}{P(w_i) P(w_j)}}{-\ln (P(w_i, w_j) + \epsilon)}$$

Where $\text{NPMI} \in [-1, +1]$. Positive values denote semantic alignment, while negative values reflect semantic noise or unaligned co-occurrence.

### 3.3 Qualitative Analysis
- **Why Classical LDA Failed:** LDA relies on document-level Bag-of-Words co-occurrence. Across 1,000 multilingual comments, cross-lingual vocabulary fragmentation causes severe topic intrusion, grouping common stopwords (`the`, `और`, `hain`, `nahi`) across multiple clusters.
- **Why Advanced c-TF-IDF Succeeded:** Sublinear TF-IDF combined with TruncatedSVD dimensionality reduction and Agglomerative Clustering groups semantically related comments into compact clusters. Class-based TF-IDF (c-TF-IDF) extracts keywords that are specific to each cluster relative to the global corpus, driving mean NPMI from -0.2666 to +0.6352.

## 5. Outlier Novelty Threshold ($\tau$) Ablation Study

### 5.1 Real Threshold Ablation Sweep
Novelty detection flags potential emergent topics when a comment's semantic distance to the nearest topic cluster centroid satisfies $d_i = 1.0 - \max_k \cos(e_i, c_k) \ge \tau$.

We performed a parameter sweep across $\tau \in [0.40, 0.70]$ (step 0.05) evaluated against the 118 ground-truth emergent drift records (`is_emergent_drift = True`):

| Threshold ($\tau$) | Precision | Recall | F1-Score | True Positives (TP) | False Positives (FP) | False Negatives (FN) | True Negatives (TN) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **$\tau = 0.40$** | **0.0922** | **0.5424** | **0.1576** | **64** | 630 | 54 | 252 |
| **$\tau = 0.45$** | 0.0642 | 0.3136 | 0.1066 | 37 | 539 | 81 | 343 |
| **$\tau = 0.50$** | 0.0552 | 0.2458 | 0.0902 | 29 | 496 | 89 | 386 |
| **$\tau = 0.55$** | 0.0414 | 0.1610 | 0.0659 | 19 | 440 | 99 | 442 |
| **$\tau = 0.60$** | 0.0480 | 0.1610 | 0.0739 | 19 | 377 | 99 | 505 |
| **$\tau = 0.65$** | 0.0443 | 0.1186 | 0.0645 | 14 | 302 | 104 | 580 |
| **$\tau = 0.70$** | 0.0513 | 0.1186 | 0.0716 | 14 | 259 | 104 | 623 |

### 5.2 Operating Point Calibration
- **Empirical Optimum:** $\tau = 0.40$ achieves the highest harmonic mean (**F1 = 0.1576**), recovering **54.2%** of all emergent drift comments ($64 / 118$).
- **Single Source of Truth:** The previously claimed F1 of 0.852 at $\tau=0.55$ was uncalibrated. Under empirical measurement, $\tau = 0.55$ yields F1 = 0.0659 with only 16.1% recall.
- Based on this ablation, the single repository-wide default threshold has been unified in [`src/config.py`](file:///c:/Users/itsmo/Documents/capstone_course_feedback/src/config.py) at **$\tau = 0.40$**.

## 6. Comprehensive Error Analysis & Post-Mortem

### Failure Mode 1: Code-Mixed False Negatives in LID
- **Input:** Short code-mixed comments like `"Paper pattern bohot tough tha"`
- **Outcome:** Classified as `en` if distinct Hindi markers are fewer than 2.
- **Root Cause:** Script detection flags Latin; rule-based code-mixing requires $\ge 2$ lexical markers to avoid false positives on words like *"the"* / *"par"*.
- **Mitigation:** Lowering the threshold to 1 marker would increase Hinglish recall from 61.0% to ~78%, but at the expense of introducing false positives into pure English feedback. The current high precision (100%) was deemed safer for institutional categorization.

### Failure Mode 2: Multi-Topic Clauses Without Strong Connectives
- **Input:** `"Prof explains well lab machines crash continuously."`
- **Outcome:** Both clauses may get attributed to `TOPIC_1` if the clause splitter does not detect explicit coordinating conjunctions (e.g. `but`, `par`, `lekin`).
- **Mitigation:** Enhanced the clause splitter with punctuation and secondary clause segmenters to handle unpunctuated transitions.

### Failure Mode 3: Outlier Novelty False Positives at Low Thresholds
- **Input:** Highly idiosyncratic student comments mentioning unusual electives or personal equipment.
- **Outcome:** Flagged as novelty outliers ($d_i \ge 0.40$).
- **Mitigation:** The system pairs pointwise thresholding with the [`EmergingTopicClusterer`](file:///c:/Users/itsmo/Documents/capstone_course_feedback/src/innovation/emerging_clusterer.py) and Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Governance gate. Individual false positives never mutate the institutional topic catalog unless they form a cohesive cluster ($N \ge 15$) approved by an administrator.
